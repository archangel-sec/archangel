# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

## Summary

- Be respectful, inclusive, and constructive.
- Disagree about technical decisions; do not attack people.
- Harassment in any form is not tolerated.
- This is a security-critical project; bad-faith engagement (vague threats of vulnerabilities, demands without evidence, deliberate noise) is treated as a conduct issue.

## Enforcement

Reports to `conduct@archangel-sec.org` (mailbox provisioned with the domain). Maintainers reserve the right to remove contributions and ban participants who violate this code.

## Why a separate document

Because security work brings adversarial framing. We want to make clear: adversarial framing is welcome in **threat modeling** and **review of proposals**. It is not welcome in interactions with **people**.
